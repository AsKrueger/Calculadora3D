## Full description (max. 4000)

```
How much does each 3D print really cost you, and what should you charge? 3D Cost Manager tells you in seconds, including everything most calculators forget.

★ THE REAL COST, NOT JUST FILAMENT
• Material: several colours per piece, purge and waste
• Electricity: your printer's average power and your kWh price
• Printer wear and maintenance
• Your time: setup per plate and finishing per piece
• Extras: magnets, inserts, boxes…
• Failed prints: only what gets reprinted is counted again
• Several pieces per plate

★ WHAT TO CHARGE ON EACH CHANNEL
Set your margin, the profit you want or a fixed price. The app adds Etsy, Amazon, eBay or your shop's fees and the VAT, and shows what you actually keep. The channel comparison shows the price on all of them at once.

★ IMPORT FROM YOUR SLICER
Open or share a G-code or 3MF (Flash Studio, OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura) and print time, grams per filament and the part picture are filled in.

★ QUOTES AND ORDERS
• Quotes with client, quantities, discount and shipping
• PDF with your logo, ready for WhatsApp or email
• Statuses: draft, sent, accepted, printed, delivered
• Monthly summary and a sales and profit chart
• Export to spreadsheet (CSV)

★ FOR EVERY DAY
• Print timer that notifies you when the print is done
• Spool stock with a low-filament warning
• Filament library: Bambu Lab, Flashforge, Sunlu, eSun, Polymaker, Prusament…
• Printer templates: Flashforge, Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic

★ YOUR DATA IS YOURS
No account, works offline: everything stays on your phone. Back up to a file whenever you want.

Free, with a small banner at the top you can remove with a one-time payment. No subscriptions.
Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.
```
